Questions

Before you come in.

How do I know what width to get?

Width changes the look more than anything else. If you're unsure, come in — seeing an 8 mm next to an 11 mm on your own wrist settles it in about ten seconds.

Are the weights on the site accurate?

They come directly from Monaci for each exact size and SKU, and are shown as supplied rather than rounded or estimated. Final specifications are confirmed by the store.

Why isn’t the price shown on the site?

Cuban pricing is driven by the weight of the piece and the current gold market, both of which move. Rather than publish a number that may be stale by the time you read it, we quote your exact configuration when you call or text. The builder gives you the precise weight and SKU so there is no guesswork.

What if I want a size Monaci doesn't make?

The listed sizes are the ones Monaci produces. Outside that range we make Cuban bracelets and chains to custom widths and lengths in our own shop — tell us what you're after.

Can I see it before I buy?

Come into the store. Cuban links are one of those things photographs consistently misrepresent, in both directions.
