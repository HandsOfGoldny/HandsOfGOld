Questions

Before you come in.

What's the difference between a lab diamond and a natural one?

A lab-grown diamond has the same chemical and optical properties as a mined one — it is a diamond, not a simulant. The practical difference is cost per carat, which is generally lower, so the same budget usually buys a larger stone or a better setting. We'll show you both.

How much should I spend?

There is no correct number, and old rules about months of salary are marketing. Tell us a figure you're comfortable with and we'll show you the best ring at it.

Can I use a stone that's already in the family?

Yes. Bring it in — we'll check the stone and talk through settings that suit it.

Can the ring be sized and engraved?

Yes. Sizing and inside-band laser engraving are both done in-house.

Do you offer financing?

We work with third-party financing and lease-to-own providers. Eligibility, approval and provider terms apply — ask in store or see the payment options on our homepage.
