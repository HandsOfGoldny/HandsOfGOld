What we repair

Bring it in — most of it is done here, today.

Repairs are handled in-house by our own jeweler. That means no shipping your piece to a third party, and a straight answer on what it needs before any work starts.

Ring Sizing

Sizing up or down in gold, white gold and platinum, including comfort-fit and stone-set bands. We check the setting and prongs while the ring is on the bench.

Chain & Clasp Repair

Broken links, snapped chains, worn clasps and lobster replacements on Cuban, rope, figaro, box and specialty chains.

Watch Service & Batteries

Battery replacement, band and link sizing, pin and pushpin work, and pressure-fit or screw-back case closing.

Restorations

Tired, bent or damaged pieces cleaned up, straightened, re-soldered and refinished so they can go back into rotation.

Stone Tightening & Checks

Loose stones re-tightened and prongs inspected before they become a lost stone. Bring it in if something rattles or catches.

Polishing & Refinishing

Scratches, dullness and everyday wear polished back on gold and silver pieces, with the original finish kept in mind.

Questions

Before you come in.

How long does a repair take?

Most common repairs — sizing, clasp work, batteries — are done in 30 minutes or less while you wait. Turnaround varies by repair type, parts and the condition of the piece, so we give you a realistic time once we've looked at it.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in during store hours and we'll look at the piece with you at the counter.

Will you tell me the price before you start?

Yes. Our jeweler assesses the piece first and explains the work and the cost. Nothing is started until you approve it.

Do you repair pieces that weren't bought here?

Yes. Bring in any piece and we'll tell you what it needs.

Can you fix a chain that's broken in several places?

Often, yes. Bring it in and we'll tell you honestly whether the repair is worth it or whether the chain is better replaced.
