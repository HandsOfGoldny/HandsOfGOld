Gold-buying calculator

The website gold-buying calculator provides a non-binding online estimate using the live gold spot price available to the site, the purity selected by the visitor, the weight entered by the visitor, and our current gold purchase rate. The calculator is not an appraisal, guaranteed quote, or promise to purchase an item for the displayed amount.

Actual in-store purchase prices may differ after the item is tested and weighed. Stones, solder, clasps, watch parts, non-gold components, and other materials may not count toward payable gold weight. Market prices can change between the online estimate and an in-store transaction.

Applicable Suffolk County rules govern in-store precious-metal purchases by weight, including pennyweight-based weight and price-per-pennyweight disclosures. The online calculator does not replace any required in-store testing, weighing, posted notices, receipts, records, identification, or other transaction requirements.

The Custom Jewelry Studio is a planning and request tool. Using the Studio or copying the summary does not by itself send those design details to Hands of Gold.
