How it's made

Golden hands, start to finish.

Real work from our bench, step by step, from your mold to a set with 251 individually placed stones.

  1. Step 1

    It starts at our bench.

    Your mold becomes a model of your teeth, detailed by hand on our jeweler's bench, one tooth edge at a time.

  2. Step 2

    We scan your mold.

    Your impression is scanned in 3D, then we trace every tooth edge and the gum line by hand. Your set follows your bite, not a template.

  3. Step 3

    We design it stone by stone.

    Every stone is placed one at a time. This set has 251 stones in four sizes, 1.1 to 1.7 mm, mapped onto your scan. We also calculate the metal weight in every karat before any gold is used, so you can see what goes into your set.

    Tap the image to see the full report.

  4. Step 4

    You try it on in plastic first.

    We print your design in plastic at 0.1–0.13 mm detail and you get these fitment samples free of charge, so we can check the fit and comfort before any metal is used. They're for fit only, not the finished grillz.

  5. Design render
    Step 5

    You see it before it's made.

    You approve the finished look before anything is made in metal. Design render shown, not a photo of the finished piece.

Fitment samples

Check the fit before the metal.

During the process, you'll receive plastic versions of the teeth strictly for fitment purposes. They're provided free of charge and are not the finished grillz.

7-day fit guarantee: if your finished grillz don't fit right, let us know within 7 days of pickup or delivery and we'll adjust them at no charge. For mail-in orders, we split the cost of standard ground shipping for the adjustment with you 50/50, or you can pay extra for expedited shipping.

How it works

Two ways to get fitted.

Come in for your impression, or take it at home with our mail-in kit. Either way, your set is made from your own impressions.

In store

Visit us in Copiague

About 7 days for plain and polished sets

  1. Reserve your set with a deposit of $125 or more, or just come in.
  2. We take your impressions at 494 Oak Street, Copiague.
  3. We design and handcraft your set in-house.
  4. Pick up your grillz.

Custom, gemstone and one-of-a-kind designs: timing is confirmed with your quote.

Mail-in

Fitted from anywhere

About 3–4 weeks · expedited shipping available

  1. Reserve your set with a deposit of $125 or more. It covers your $49.95 mold kit and, in most cases, shipping. The rest goes toward your set. If shipping costs more, we'll contact you about the difference before your kit is sent.
  2. Receive your impression kit with detailed, step-by-step instructions.
  3. Take your impressions at home and mail them back to us.
  4. We design and handcraft your set in-house.
  5. Your grillz are shipped to you. Ask about expedited shipping.
