FAQ

Monaci Cuban questions

How is the price calculated?

Every Monaci price is calculated straight from the actual live spot price of gold, adjusted for the gold content of the karat (58.5% for 14K, 41.7% for 10K) and the exact catalog weight. That is our best possible price, and when gold goes down, our prices go down with it.

Why does the price expire after 25 minutes?

Gold trades all day, so a price on this page is held for 25 minutes. After that, tap Get Today's Price for the current number.

Are Monaci Cubans solid or hollow?

Every Monaci Cuban on this page is hollow 14K or 10K gold. Hollow links give you the full, bold Cuban look at a lighter weight and a lower price. The catalog weight shown is the gold weight your price is based on.

Can I buy online?

Yes. Tap Buy Now to check out through Stripe. Pay online, then pick up in store at 494 Oak Street, Copiague, within 7 days. New York sales tax is added at checkout. Bring a photo ID that matches the card used, and we'll call you when your piece is ready. See our Returns & Exchanges policy.

Why are some karats disabled?

The catalog headings include 10K, 14K, 18K, 21K and 22K, but the supplied pages only contain populated weights for 14K and selected 10K configurations. Missing weights are not guessed.

Can I send my exact configuration to the store?

Yes. Use the Text Us This Piece button or share the configuration link. The URL preserves your bracelet/necklace, width, length and karat selections.
