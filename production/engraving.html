Questions

Before you come in.

What metals can you engrave?

Gold, silver, platinum, stainless steel and titanium are all commonly engraved. Bring the piece in and we'll confirm how that specific surface and finish will take a mark.

Can I bring in a piece I already own?

Yes. Bring it in and we'll look at the surface, the finish and the space available before quoting anything.

Is laser engraving permanent?

Laser engraving marks the metal itself rather than sitting on the surface, so it stands up to normal wear far better than printed or lightly etched text.

Can you engrave a logo?

Often, yes — it depends on how much fine detail the design has and how small it needs to be. Send us the artwork and we'll tell you honestly how it will come out.

How long does engraving take?

It depends on the piece, the amount of text and how busy the bench is. We give you a realistic turnaround once we've seen the item.
